Looking prompts

  1. Trace the movement from the ground at Luke's feet up to the vision above his head. What links the earthly and heavenly zones?
  2. Find the prophets and angels in the circles, and the stack of books on Luke's lap. How does the inscription explain them?
  3. Mark where the painter uses gold, frontal poses and enlarged gestures instead of naturalistic space.
  4. The columns and arch recall Roman and Carolingian frames. Label the parts and note what they add.
